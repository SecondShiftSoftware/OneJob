# One Job privacy policy

**Draft.** Last updated 24 October 2026.

One Job is an iPhone app that hands you one household maintenance job at a time. It's made by Ben Hoare-Ward, an independent developer in Australia ("I" and "me" below).

The short version: **One Job doesn't collect your data.** Everything you put into the app stays on your iPhone. I can't see it, and nothing is sent to me or to anyone else.

## What the app stores

To do its job, One Job keeps the following on your iPhone:

- The things in your house and their groups, such as "Aircon" in "Inside". For a vehicle or pet, this can include its name, type, make and model, and dates such as a rego due date.
- Your jobs, how often they're due, and when each one was last done.
- Your completion history, including any notes you add.
- Your settings, such as your available days, date changes, reminder times and hemisphere.

This data is stored in the app's own storage on your device. The One Job widgets read the same data through a shared space on your phone (an App Group) so they can show today's job and mark it done. That shared space is also on your device only.

## What the app doesn't do

- It doesn't send your data to me or to any server. The app makes no network connections of its own.
- It has no accounts or sign-in.
- It has no advertising, analytics or tracking, and contains no third-party code that does.
- It doesn't ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.
- It doesn't sell or share your data, because it never has it.

## Reminders

One Job's reminders, such as the morning nudge and follow-ups, are local notifications. They're scheduled on your iPhone by iOS and don't pass through any server. You can turn them off in the app or in the iPhone's Settings at any time.

## Backups and exporting

You can export a backup file from Settings. The file contains your One Job data, and it goes wherever you choose to send or save it, such as the Files app, AirDrop or an email. Once it leaves the app, it's handled by the service you chose and that service's privacy policy applies. I never receive a copy unless you send one to me yourself.

Your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backups may include One Job's data, the same as any other app. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you.

## Information from Apple

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give me crash reports and general usage figures, such as how many people installed the app. Apple removes information that identifies you before I see it. You can turn this off at any time in the same setting.

If you download One Job from the App Store or TestFlight, Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. If you're a TestFlight tester, Apple shares your name and email address with me so I can invite you, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send through TestFlight come to me.

## Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your device, you're in control of it. Deleting One Job from your iPhone removes its data and its widgets' data. Any backup files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them.

## Children

One Job isn't aimed at children, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

Future versions of One Job may add features that change how data is handled, such as syncing your data across your own devices through your private iCloud account, or sharing a household's jobs between people. If that happens, I'll update this policy before the feature is released and change the date at the top. The app won't start sending your data anywhere new without telling you first.
